# AI-Driven Medical Diagnosis System

This advanced platform enables healthcare professionals and users to upload medical images such as MRI scans, CT scans, and X-rays to receive detailed insights into potential health conditions.

## Features

- **Image Upload**: Supports uploading MRI images, CT scans, and X-ray files.
- **Automated Diagnosis**: Analyzes images to identify potential conditions such as Arthritis, Alzheimer’s Disease, Brain Tumors, and more.
- **Disease Staging**: Assesses the stage or severity of diagnosed conditions.
- **Preventive Strategies**: Provides personalized recommendations to prevent or manage health risks.
- **Lifestyle & Dietary Guidance**: Offers suggestions for dietary changes and lifestyle modifications to support overall health and wellbeing.

## How It Works

1. Upload your medical images to the platform.
2. The system processes the images using AI models trained on diverse datasets.
3. Receive a comprehensive report including potential diagnoses, stages, prevention tips, and diet recommendations.
4. Use the insights to facilitate early intervention or consult healthcare professionals for further action.
